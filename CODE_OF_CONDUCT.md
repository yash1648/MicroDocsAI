# Code of Conduct

We strive to maintain a respectful, collaborative community.

## Our Pledge
- Be welcoming and inclusive.
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; disagree without being disagreeable.

## Expected Behavior
- Use clear, kind language.
- Provide helpful feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
- Report violations privately to maintainers.
- Maintainers may take appropriate action.
